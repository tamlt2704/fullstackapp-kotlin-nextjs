# Chinese HSK 1 - Complete Beginner Guide

*Published on December 13, 2024*

## Overview

**HSK 1** is the first level of the Chinese Proficiency Test. At this level, you can understand and use simple Chinese phrases, meet basic communication needs, and possess the ability to further your Chinese studies.

### Requirements
- **Vocabulary**: 150 words
- **Grammar**: Basic sentence structures
- **Characters**: Recognize and write 150 characters
- **Test Duration**: 40 minutes (Listening 20 min, Reading 20 min)

---

## Part 1: Pinyin Basics

### What is Pinyin?
Pinyin is the romanization system for Chinese characters. Master this before learning characters!

### Initials (声母 - Shēngmǔ)
```
b  p  m  f
d  t  n  l
g  k  h
j  q  x
zh ch sh r
z  c  s
y  w
```

### Finals (韵母 - Yùnmǔ)
```
a  o  e  i  u  ü
ai ei ui ao ou iu ie üe er
an en in un ün
ang eng ing ong
```

### Four Tones
- **1st Tone (ā)**: High and flat ¯
- **2nd Tone (á)**: Rising /
- **3rd Tone (ǎ)**: Falling then rising ∨
- **4th Tone (à)**: Falling \
- **Neutral Tone (a)**: Light and short

**Practice**: 
- mā (妈 mother) - má (麻 hemp) - mǎ (马 horse) - mà (骂 scold)

## Part 3: Essential Grammar

### 1. Basic Sentence Structure: Subject + Verb + Object
```
我 吃 米饭。
Wǒ chī mǐfàn.
I eat rice.

他 看 电影。
Tā kàn diànyǐng.
He watches movies.
```

### 2. 是 (shì) - "to be"
```
我 是 学生。
Wǒ shì xuésheng.
I am a student.

这 是 我的 书。
Zhè shì wǒ de shū.
This is my book.
```

### 3. Possessive: 的 (de)
```
我的 妈妈
wǒ de māma
my mother

他的 电脑
tā de diànnǎo
his computer
```

### 4. Yes/No Questions: 吗 (ma)
```
你 是 学生 吗？
Nǐ shì xuésheng ma?
Are you a student?

你 喜欢 猫 吗？
Nǐ xǐhuan māo ma?
Do you like cats?
```

### 5. Negation: 不 (bù) and 没 (méi)
```
我 不 是 老师。
Wǒ bù shì lǎoshī.
I am not a teacher.

我 没有 钱。
Wǒ méiyǒu qián.
I don't have money.
```

### 6. 有 (yǒu) - "to have"
```
我 有 一个 哥哥。
Wǒ yǒu yí gè gēge.
I have an older brother.

他 有 三本 书。
Tā yǒu sān běn shū.
He has three books.
```

### 7. Location: 在 (zài)
```
我 在 家。
Wǒ zài jiā.
I am at home.

书 在 桌子 上。
Shū zài zhuōzi shàng.
The book is on the table.
```

### 8. Measure Words
```
一个人 (yí gè rén) - one person
两本书 (liǎng běn shū) - two books
三杯茶 (sān bēi chá) - three cups of tea
```

### 9. Time Expression
```
我 今天 很 忙。
Wǒ jīntiān hěn máng.
I am very busy today.

现在 几点？
Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?
What time is it now?
```

### 10. 都 (dōu) - "all, both"
```
我们 都 是 学生。
Wǒmen dōu shì xuésheng.
We are all students.
```

## Part 4: Common Phrases & Dialogues

### Greetings
```
A: 你好！
   Nǐ hǎo!
   Hello!

B: 你好！
   Nǐ hǎo!
   Hello!

A: 你叫什么名字？
   Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
   What's your name?

B: 我叫李明。你呢？
   Wǒ jiào Lǐ Míng. Nǐ ne?
   My name is Li Ming. And you?

A: 我叫王红。认识你很高兴。
   Wǒ jiào Wáng Hóng. Rènshi nǐ hěn gāoxìng.
   My name is Wang Hong. Nice to meet you.
```

### Shopping
```
A: 这个多少钱？
   Zhège duōshao qián?
   How much is this?

B: 五十块。
   Wǔshí kuài.
   50 yuan.

A: 太贵了。
   Tài guì le.
   Too expensive.

B: 那个便宜，三十块。
   Nàge piányi, sānshí kuài.
   That one is cheap, 30 yuan.
```

### At Restaurant
```
A: 你想吃什么？
   Nǐ xiǎng chī shénme?
   What do you want to eat?

B: 我想吃米饭和菜。
   Wǒ xiǎng chī mǐfàn hé cài.
   I want to eat rice and vegetables.

A: 你喝茶吗？
   Nǐ hē chá ma?
   Do you drink tea?

B: 不，我喝水。
   Bù, wǒ hē shuǐ.
   No, I drink water.
```

## Part 6: Study Tips

### Daily Study Plan (4-6 weeks)

**Week 1-2: Pinyin & Basic Vocabulary**
- Day 1-3: Master pinyin and tones
- Day 4-7: Learn 20 words daily
- Day 8-14: Review and practice pronunciation

**Week 3-4: Grammar & Sentences**
- Learn basic sentence structures
- Practice 10 sentences daily
- Write simple dialogues

**Week 5-6: Practice & Review**
- Take practice tests
- Review weak areas
- Practice listening daily

### Learning Resources
- **Apps**: HelloChinese, Pleco, Anki
- **Websites**: ChinesePod, YoYo Chinese
- **YouTube**: Learn Chinese with Emma, ChineseFor.Us
- **Practice**: Write characters 10 times each
- **Speaking**: Record yourself and compare

### Memory Techniques
1. **Flashcards**: Use spaced repetition
2. **Mnemonics**: Create stories for characters
3. **Practice**: Use words in sentences
4. **Immersion**: Watch Chinese shows with subtitles
5. **Speaking**: Practice with language partners

## Part 7: Character Writing Guide

### Basic Strokes
1. 横 (héng) - Horizontal stroke: 一
2. 竖 (shù) - Vertical stroke: 丨
3. 撇 (piě) - Left-falling stroke: 丿
4. 捺 (nà) - Right-falling stroke: 丶
5. 点 (diǎn) - Dot: 、

### Stroke Order Rules
1. Top to bottom: 三
2. Left to right: 川
3. Horizontal before vertical: 十
4. Outside before inside: 月
5. Middle before sides: 小
6. Inside before closing: 国
