# Chinese HSK 3 - Intermediate Level Guide

*Published on December 13, 2024*

## Overview

**HSK 3** assesses your ability to communicate in Chinese at an intermediate level in daily, academic, and professional situations.

### Requirements
- **Vocabulary**: 600 words (300 new + 300 from HSK 1-2)
- **Grammar**: Complex sentence structures
- **Test Duration**: 90 minutes (Listening 35 min, Reading 30 min, Writing 25 min)
- **Passing Score**: 180/300

## Advanced Grammar Points

### 1. Resultative Complements
```
我听懂了。
Wǒ tīng dǒng le.
I understood (by listening).

他做完作业了。
Tā zuò wán zuòyè le.
He finished doing homework.
```

### 2. Directional Complements
```
他走进来了。
Tā zǒu jìnlái le.
He walked in.

书掉下去了。
Shū diào xiàqù le.
The book fell down.
```

### 3. 把 (bǎ) Construction
```
我把书放在桌子上了。
Wǒ bǎ shū fàng zài zhuōzi shàng le.
I put the book on the table.

请把门关上。
Qǐng bǎ mén guān shàng.
Please close the door.
```

### 4. 被 (bèi) Passive Voice
```
我的自行车被偷了。
Wǒ de zìxíngchē bèi tōu le.
My bicycle was stolen.

他被老师批评了。
Tā bèi lǎoshī pīpíng le.
He was criticized by the teacher.
```

### 5. 除了...以外 (chúle...yǐwài)
```
除了汉语以外，我还学英语。
Chúle Hànyǔ yǐwài, wǒ hái xué Yīngyǔ.
Besides Chinese, I also study English.
```

### 6. 不但...而且 (búdàn...érqiě)
```
他不但聪明，而且很努力。
Tā búdàn cōngming, érqiě hěn nǔlì.
He is not only smart but also hardworking.
```

### 7. 虽然...但是 (suīrán...dànshì)
```
虽然很累，但是我很高兴。
Suīrán hěn lèi, dànshì wǒ hěn gāoxìng.
Although tired, I'm very happy.
```

### 8. 如果...就 (rúguǒ...jiù)
```
如果明天下雨，我就不去了。
Rúguǒ míngtiān xià yǔ, wǒ jiù bú qù le.
If it rains tomorrow, I won't go.
```

### 9. 一...就 (yī...jiù)
```
我一回家就做作业。
Wǒ yī huí jiā jiù zuò zuòyè.
As soon as I get home, I do homework.
```

### 10. Potential Complements
```
我听得懂汉语。
Wǒ tīng de dǒng Hànyǔ.
I can understand Chinese (by listening).

这个字我写不好。
Zhège zì wǒ xiě bu hǎo.
I can't write this character well.
```

## Sample Dialogues

### Making Appointments
```
A: 你明天有时间吗？我想请你吃饭。
   Nǐ míngtiān yǒu shíjiān ma? Wǒ xiǎng qǐng nǐ chīfàn.
   Do you have time tomorrow? I'd like to treat you to dinner.

B: 对不起，明天我有事。后天怎么样？
   Duìbuqǐ, míngtiān wǒ yǒu shì. Hòutiān zěnmeyàng?
   Sorry, I'm busy tomorrow. How about the day after?

A: 后天可以。我们在哪儿见面？
   Hòutiān kěyǐ. Wǒmen zài nǎr jiànmiàn?
   The day after works. Where shall we meet?

B: 在学校门口吧，六点半。
   Zài xuéxiào ménkǒu ba, liù diǎn bàn.
   At the school gate, 6:30.
```

### Expressing Opinions
```
A: 你觉得这部电影怎么样？
   Nǐ juéde zhè bù diànyǐng zěnmeyàng?
   What do you think of this movie?

B: 我认为很有意思。虽然有点儿长，但是很感人。
   Wǒ rènwéi hěn yǒu yìsi. Suīrán yǒudiǎnr cháng, dànshì hěn gǎnrén.
   I think it's very interesting. Although a bit long, it's very touching.

A: 我同意。导演很有名，他的电影都很好。
   Wǒ tóngyì. Dǎoyǎn hěn yǒumíng, tā de diànyǐng dōu hěn hǎo.
   I agree. The director is famous, all his movies are good.
```

## Practice Questions

### Reading Comprehension
```
李明是一个大学生。他不但学习认真，而且很关心别人。
虽然他的家离学校很远，但是他每天都按时到校。
除了上课以外，他还参加了很多活动。
他希望毕业以后能找到一份好工作。
```

**Questions:**
1. 李明是什么样的学生？
   **Answer: 认真、关心别人的学生**

2. 他的家离学校远吗？
   **Answer: 很远**

3. 他除了上课还做什么？
   **Answer: 参加很多活动**

### Writing Section
**Task: Write 80-100 characters about your weekend plans**

Sample Answer:
```
这个周末我打算去爬山。虽然天气预报说可能会下雨，
但是我还是想去。我会带上雨伞和相机。
如果天气好，我就拍很多照片。
爬山不但可以锻炼身体，而且可以欣赏美丽的风景。
```

## Study Tips

### 12-Week Study Plan
- **Weeks 1-4**: Master 100 new words/week
- **Weeks 5-8**: Focus on complex grammar
- **Weeks 9-10**: Practice reading & writing
- **Weeks 11-12**: Mock tests & review

### Key Focus Areas
1. Master resultative & directional complements
2. Practice 把 and 被 constructions
3. Learn complex sentence patterns
4. Improve writing skills (80-100 characters)
5. Expand vocabulary to 600 words
