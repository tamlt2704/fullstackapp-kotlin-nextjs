## HSK 1 Grammar Points
1. **是 (shì)** - to be: 我是学生。(I am a student.)
2. **有 (yǒu)** - to have: 我有一个苹果。(I have an apple.)
3. **在 (zài)** - at/in: 我在家。(I am at home.)
4. **的 (de)** - possessive: 我的书 (my book)
5. **吗 (ma)** - question particle: 你好吗？(How are you?)
6. **呢 (ne)** - question particle: 你呢？(And you?)
7. **不 (bù)** - negation: 我不是老师。(I am not a teacher.)
8. **没 (méi)** - negation for 有: 我没有钱。(I don't have money.)
9. **很 (hěn)** - very: 很好 (very good)
10. **想 (xiǎng)** - want to: 我想去。(I want to go.)
11. **会 (huì)** - can/know how: 我会说汉语。(I can speak Chinese.)
12. **能 (néng)** - can/able to: 我能来。(I can come.)
13. **几 (jǐ)** - how many: 几点？(What time?)
14. **什么 (shénme)** - what: 这是什么？(What is this?)
15. **哪儿 (nǎr)** - where: 你去哪儿？(Where are you going?)
