# Chinese HSK 2 - Elementary Level Guide

*Published on December 13, 2024*

## Overview

**HSK 2** tests your ability to communicate in simple and routine tasks requiring a simple and direct exchange of information on familiar topics.

### Requirements
- **Vocabulary**: 300 words (150 new + 150 from HSK 1)
- **Grammar**: More complex sentence structures
- **Test Duration**: 55 minutes (Listening 25 min, Reading 25 min, Writing 5 min)
- **Passing Score**: 120/200

## Grammar Points

### 1. Past Tense: 了 (le)
```
我吃了早饭。
Wǒ chīle zǎofàn.
I ate breakfast.

他去了北京。
Tā qùle Běijīng.
He went to Beijing.
```

### 2. Progressive Aspect: 正在...呢 (zhèngzài...ne)
```
我正在看书呢。
Wǒ zhèngzài kàn shū ne.
I am reading a book.

他们正在吃饭呢。
Tāmen zhèngzài chīfàn ne.
They are eating.
```

### 3. Experience: 过 (guo)
```
我去过中国。
Wǒ qùguo Zhōngguó.
I have been to China.

你吃过北京烤鸭吗？
Nǐ chīguo Běijīng kǎoyā ma?
Have you eaten Beijing roast duck?
```

### 4. Duration: 了 (le) with time
```
我学了两年汉语了。
Wǒ xuéle liǎng nián Hànyǔ le.
I have studied Chinese for two years.

他等了一个小时了。
Tā děngle yí gè xiǎoshí le.
He has waited for one hour.
```

### 5. Comparison: 比 (bǐ)
```
我比他高。
Wǒ bǐ tā gāo.
I am taller than him.

这个比那个便宜。
Zhège bǐ nàge piányi.
This is cheaper than that.
```

### 6. Superlative: 最 (zuì)
```
他是最聪明的学生。
Tā shì zuì cōngming de xuésheng.
He is the smartest student.

这是最好的饭馆。
Zhè shì zuì hǎo de fànguǎn.
This is the best restaurant.
```

### 7. Modal Verbs: 会, 能, 可以
```
我会说汉语。(ability learned)
Wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ.
I can speak Chinese.

我能帮你。(capability)
Wǒ néng bāng nǐ.
I can help you.

我可以进来吗？(permission)
Wǒ kěyǐ jìnlái ma?
May I come in?
```

### 8. Causative: 让 (ràng)
```
老师让我们做作业。
Lǎoshī ràng wǒmen zuò zuòyè.
The teacher asks us to do homework.
```

### 9. Giving: 给 (gěi)
```
我给你一本书。
Wǒ gěi nǐ yì běn shū.
I give you a book.

他给我打电话。
Tā gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà.
He calls me.
```

### 10. Because...Therefore: 因为...所以 (yīnwèi...suǒyǐ)
```
因为下雨，所以我没去。
Yīnwèi xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒ méi qù.
Because it rained, I didn't go.
```

### 11. Time Duration
```
我学习了三个小时。
Wǒ xuéxíle sān gè xiǎoshí.
I studied for three hours.

他在中国住了两年。
Tā zài Zhōngguó zhùle liǎng nián.
He lived in China for two years.
```

### 12. Directional Complements: 来/去 (lái/qù)
```
他走进来了。
Tā zǒu jìnlái le.
He walked in.

我跑出去了。
Wǒ pǎo chūqù le.
I ran out.
```

## Common Dialogues

### At the Doctor's Office
```
A: 你哪儿不舒服？
   Nǐ nǎr bù shūfu?
   Where don't you feel well?

B: 我头疼，还发烧。
   Wǒ tóuténg, hái fāshāo.
   I have a headache and fever.

A: 你应该多休息，多喝水。
   Nǐ yīnggāi duō xiūxi, duō hē shuǐ.
   You should rest more and drink more water.

B: 我需要吃药吗？
   Wǒ xūyào chī yào ma?
   Do I need to take medicine?

A: 是的，一天三次。
   Shì de, yì tiān sān cì.
   Yes, three times a day.
```

### Making Plans
```
A: 周末你想做什么？
   Zhōumò nǐ xiǎng zuò shénme?
   What do you want to do this weekend?

B: 我想去公园。你呢？
   Wǒ xiǎng qù gōngyuán. Nǐ ne?
   I want to go to the park. How about you?

A: 我也想去。我们一起去吧。
   Wǒ yě xiǎng qù. Wǒmen yìqǐ qù ba.
   I want to go too. Let's go together.

B: 好的。几点见面？
   Hǎo de. Jǐ diǎn jiànmiàn?
   Okay. What time shall we meet?

A: 早上九点，怎么样？
   Zǎoshang jiǔ diǎn, zěnmeyàng?
   9 AM, how about that?

B: 没问题。明天见！
   Méi wèntí. Míngtiān jiàn!
   No problem. See you tomorrow!
```

### Asking for Directions
```
A: 请问，银行在哪儿？
   Qǐngwèn, yínháng zài nǎr?
   Excuse me, where is the bank?

B: 往前走，然后向右转。
   Wǎng qián zǒu, ránhòu xiàng yòu zhuǎn.
   Go straight, then turn right.

A: 远吗？
   Yuǎn ma?
   Is it far?

B: 不远，走路五分钟就到了。
   Bù yuǎn, zǒulù wǔ fēnzhōng jiù dào le.
   Not far, it's a 5-minute walk.

A: 谢谢你！
   Xièxie nǐ!
   Thank you!

B: 不客气。
   Bú kèqi.
   You're welcome.
```

## Practice Questions

### Listening Section

**Part 1 (Q1-10): Listen and judge True/False**

1. Audio: "Wǒ měitiān zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng." (我每天早上七点起床。)
   Statement: The person gets up at 7 AM every morning.
   **Answer: True**

2. Audio: "Tā bǐ wǒ gāo." (他比我高。)
   Statement: He is shorter than me.
   **Answer: False**

**Part 2 (Q11-20): Listen to dialogue and answer**

3. A: 你去过北京吗？
   B: 去过，我去年去的。
   Question: When did B go to Beijing?
   - A) This year  B) Last year  C) Next year
   **Answer: B**

### Reading Section

**Part 1 (Q21-25): Choose the correct word**

4. 我___学了两年汉语了。
   - A) 已经  B) 还  C) 就
   **Answer: A**

5. 因为下雨，___我没去。
   - A) 但是  B) 所以  C) 还是
   **Answer: B**

**Part 2 (Q26-30): Put words in correct order**

6. 比 / 他 / 我 / 高
   **Answer: 他比我高。(Tā bǐ wǒ gāo.)**

7. 正在 / 我 / 看书 / 呢
   **Answer: 我正在看书呢。(Wǒ zhèngzài kàn shū ne.)**

**Part 3 (Q31-40): Reading comprehension**

Read the passage:
```
我叫李明，今年二十岁。我是大学生。我每天早上七点起床，
八点去学校。我很喜欢学习汉语。我已经学了两年了。
我觉得汉语很有意思，但是有点儿难。我的老师很好，
常常帮助我。周末我喜欢和朋友一起去公园玩。
```

8. 李明几点起床？
   - A) 六点  B) 七点  C) 八点
   **Answer: B**

9. 李明学了多长时间汉语？
   - A) 一年  B) 两年  C) 三年
   **Answer: B**

10. 李明觉得汉语怎么样？
    - A) 很容易  B) 有意思但有点儿难  C) 不好
    **Answer: B**

## Study Tips for HSK 2

### Daily Practice (6-8 weeks)

**Week 1-2: Vocabulary Building**
- Learn 15-20 new words daily
- Review HSK 1 vocabulary
- Practice with flashcards

**Week 3-4: Grammar Focus**
- Master 了, 过, 着 particles
- Practice comparison sentences
- Learn modal verbs

**Week 5-6: Listening & Reading**
- Listen to HSK 2 audio daily
- Read simple Chinese texts
- Practice with mock tests

**Week 7-8: Writing & Review**
- Practice writing sentences
- Take full practice tests
- Review weak areas

### Recommended Resources
- **Apps**: HelloChinese (HSK 2), ChineseSkill
- **Books**: HSK Standard Course 2
- **Podcasts**: ChinesePod (Newbie level)
- **YouTube**: Yoyo Chinese HSK 2, Mandarin Corner

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. Confusing 了 (le) and 过 (guo)
2. Wrong word order in comparison sentences
3. Forgetting measure words
4. Incorrect use of 在 vs 正在
5. Mixing up 因为 and 所以 placement
