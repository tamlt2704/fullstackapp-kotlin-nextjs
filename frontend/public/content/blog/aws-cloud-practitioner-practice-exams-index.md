## Exam Tips

### Before the Exam
- Get 8 hours of sleep
- Arrive 15 minutes early
- Bring two forms of ID
- Review key concepts (not cramming)

### During the Exam
- Read questions carefully
- Eliminate wrong answers first
- Flag difficult questions for review
- Manage time (~1.5 min per question)
- Don't leave questions blank

### Question Strategies
- Look for keywords: "most cost-effective", "least operational overhead", "highest availability"
- Understand "managed" vs "unmanaged" services
- Know when to use each service
- Eliminate obviously wrong answers
