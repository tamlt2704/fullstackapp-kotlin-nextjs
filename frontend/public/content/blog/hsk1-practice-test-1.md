# HSK 1 Practice Test 1
## 40 Questions - 35 Minutes

### Part 1: Listening (20 questions)

#### Section 1 (Questions 1-5)
Listen and judge if the picture matches what you hear. Mark ✓ for correct, ✗ for incorrect.

**Q1:** 他喝水。(Tā hē shuǐ.) - Picture: Man drinking water
- Answer: ✓

**Q2:** 她看书。(Tā kàn shū.) - Picture: Woman watching TV
- Answer: ✗

**Q3:** 我吃饭。(Wǒ chī fàn.) - Picture: Person eating rice
- Answer: ✓

**Q4:** 他是学生。(Tā shì xuésheng.) - Picture: Man in business suit
- Answer: ✗

**Q5:** 今天很冷。(Jīntiān hěn lěng.) - Picture: Sunny hot day
- Answer: ✗

#### Section 2 (Questions 6-10)
Listen and select the picture that matches what you hear.

**Q6:** 我有一个苹果。(Wǒ yǒu yí gè píngguǒ.)
- A) Banana B) Apple C) Orange
- Answer: B

**Q7:** 她在家里。(Tā zài jiā lǐ.)
- A) School B) Hospital C) Home
- Answer: C

**Q8:** 他坐出租车。(Tā zuò chūzūchē.)
- A) Bus B) Taxi C) Bicycle
- Answer: B

**Q9:** 我买了一本书。(Wǒ mǎi le yì běn shū.)
- A) Book B) Pen C) Computer
- Answer: A

**Q10:** 她的猫很小。(Tā de māo hěn xiǎo.)
- A) Big dog B) Small cat C) Bird
- Answer: B

#### Section 3 (Questions 11-15)
Listen to the dialogue and select the correct picture.

**Q11:** A: 你去哪儿？(Nǐ qù nǎr?) B: 我去学校。(Wǒ qù xuéxiào.)
- A) Hospital B) School C) Store
- Answer: B

**Q12:** A: 这是什么？(Zhè shì shénme?) B: 这是茶。(Zhè shì chá.)
- A) Coffee B) Water C) Tea
- Answer: C

**Q13:** A: 你叫什么名字？(Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?) B: 我叫李明。(Wǒ jiào Lǐ Míng.)
- A) Wang Wei B) Li Ming C) Zhang San
- Answer: B

**Q14:** A: 现在几点？(Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?) B: 十二点。(Shí'èr diǎn.)
- A) 10:00 B) 11:00 C) 12:00
- Answer: C

**Q15:** A: 你喜欢什么？(Nǐ xǐhuan shénme?) B: 我喜欢狗。(Wǒ xǐhuan gǒu.)
- A) Cat B) Dog C) Fish
- Answer: B

#### Section 4 (Questions 16-20)
Listen and answer the question.

**Q16:** 她是谁？(Tā shì shéi?) - Recording: 她是我妈妈。(Tā shì wǒ māma.)
- A) Teacher B) Mother C) Friend
- Answer: B

**Q17:** 他在哪儿工作？(Tā zài nǎr gōngzuò?) - Recording: 他在医院工作。(Tā zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.)
- A) School B) Hospital C) Company
- Answer: B

**Q18:** 今天天气怎么样？(Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?) - Recording: 今天很热。(Jīntiān hěn rè.)
- A) Cold B) Hot C) Rainy
- Answer: B

**Q19:** 他有几个孩子？(Tā yǒu jǐ gè háizi?) - Recording: 他有两个孩子。(Tā yǒu liǎng gè háizi.)
- A) 1 B) 2 C) 3
- Answer: B

**Q20:** 她会说汉语吗？(Tā huì shuō Hànyǔ ma?) - Recording: 会，她会说汉语。(Huì, tā huì shuō Hànyǔ.)
- A) Yes B) No C) Maybe
- Answer: A
