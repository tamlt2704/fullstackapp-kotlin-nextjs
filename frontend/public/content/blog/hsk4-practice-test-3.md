## Test-Taking Strategies
1. **Master 1200 HSK4 words** - Vocabulary is foundation
2. **Understand complex grammar** - Focus on advanced patterns
3. **Practice listening speed** - HSK4 is faster
4. **Read extensively** - Improve comprehension
5. **Learn collocations** - Words appear in patterns
6. **Time management** - 105 minutes for 100 questions
7. **Context analysis** - Use surrounding clues
8. **Writing practice** - Character accuracy matters
9. **Review systematically** - Focus on weak areas
10. **Stay calm** - Longer test requires focus
